## 1. [4 points]

Let $R=\{(x,y)\mid x-y+2>0,\ x-y-2<0\}$ be a relation on the set of real numbers. Determine whether the relation $R$ is reflexive, symmetric, antisymmetric, and/or transitive. **Give reasons.**

<Solution>
    - 自反，理由是 $x-x+2>0$ 且 $x-x-2<0$。
    - 对称，理由是：若 $x-y+2>0$，则左式移动到右边后可推出 $0>y-x-2$；若 $x-y-2<0$，可推出 $0<y-x+2$。所以是对称的。
</Solution>

## 2. [15 points]

Let $A=\{1,2,3\}$,

1. Give all equivalence relations on $A$. (5 points)
2. Define a binary relation $R$ on $A\times A$, $\forall (u,v),(x,y)\in A\times A$, $(u,v)R(x,y) \iff u+y=x+v$. Prove that $R$ is an equivalence relation on $A\times A$. (5 points)
3. Determine the partition induced by $R$ on $A\times A$. (5 points)

<Solution>
    1. $\{\{1\},\{2\},\{3\}\};\quad \{\{1,2\},\{3\}\};\quad \{\{1,3\},\{2\}\};\quad \{\{2,3\},\{1\}\};\quad \{\{1,2,3\}\}$

    2. 等价关系从 1）导出，每个 1 分，共 5 个
       - **自反性**：对任意 $(u,v)\in A\times A$，有 $u+v=u+v$，故 $(u,v)R(u,v)$。
       - **对称性**：设 $(u,v)R(x,y)$，即 $u+y=x+v$。等式两边交换即为 $x+v=u+y$，也就是 $x+y$ 与 $u+v$ 的关系对称成立，故 $(x,y)R(u,v)$。
       - **传递性**：设 $(u,v)R(x,y)$ 且 $(x,y)R(s,t)$，则

         $$u-v=x-y \text{ 且 } x-y=s-t \implies u-v=s-t \implies u+t=s+v$$

         故 $(u,v)R(s,t)$。

       三条性质都成立，所以 $R$ 是 $A\times A$ 上的等价关系。

    3. 划分：

       $$\{\{(3,1)\},\ \{(2,1),(3,2)\},\ \{(1,1),(2,2),(3,3)\},\ \{(1,2),(2,3)\},\ \{(1,3)\}\}$$
</Solution>

## 5. [10 points]

Let $G=\{1,2,3,4\}$. Define binary operation $\odot$ by $a\odot b=(a\cdot b)\bmod 5$, $\forall a,b\in G$, $\cdot$ is the ordinary multiplication. Prove that $(G,\odot)$ is an Abelian group.

<Solution>
    1. Closure: For $a,b\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, $\gcd(ab,5)=1$, so $(ab)\bmod 5\in\{1,2,3,4\}$.
    2. Associativity: Integer multiplication mod 5 is associative.
    3. Identity: $e=1$.
    4. Inverses: $1^{-1}=1,\ 2^{-1}=3,\ 3^{-1}=2,\ 4^{-1}=4$.
    5. Commutativity: $a\odot b=(ab)\bmod 5=(ba)\bmod 5=b\odot a$ for all $a,b\in G$.

    Thus $(G,\odot)$ is an Abelian group. (2 points for each step.)
</Solution>

Operation $*$ on $S$:

Operation $\cdot$ on $T$:

$$
\begin{array}{c|cc}

<Solution>

    $$
    \begin{array}{c|cccc}
    \odot & (a,u) & (a,v) & (b,u) & (b,v) \\
    \hline
    (a,u) & (a,u) & (a,u) & (b,u) & (b,u) \\
    (a,v) & (a,u) & (a,v) & (b,u) & (b,v) \\
    (b,u) & (b,u) & (b,u) & (a,u) & (a,u) \\
    (b,v) & (b,u) & (b,v) & (a,u) & (a,v)
    \end{array}

    **Step 2** $S\times T$ is not isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_4$. (4 points)

## 7. [12 points]

Let $G=(\mathbb{Z}_6,+_6)$, the additive group of integers modulo 6. Define a binary relation $R$ on $G$ by $x\,R\,y \iff 2x\equiv 2y\pmod{6}$.

<Solution>
    1. Prove $R$ is a congruence. (4 points)

       - **Reflexivity:** For any $x\in\mathbb{Z}_6$, $2x\equiv 2x\pmod{6}$, so $x\,R\,x$.
       - **Symmetry:** If $x\,R\,y$, then $2x\equiv 2y\pmod{6}$, hence $2y\equiv 2x\pmod{6}$, so $y\,R\,x$.
       - **Transitivity:** If $x\,R\,y$ and $y\,R\,z$, then $2x\equiv 2y$ and $2y\equiv 2z\pmod{6}$; by transitivity of congruence modulo 6, $2x\equiv 2z\pmod{6}$, so $x\,R\,z$. Hence $R$ is an equivalence relation.
       - **Compatibility (congruence):** Suppose $a\,R\,b$ and $c\,R\,d$. Then $2a\equiv 2b$ and $2c\equiv 2d\pmod{6}$. Adding gives $2(a+c)\equiv 2(b+d)\pmod{6}$, i.e., $a+c\,R\,b+d$. Therefore $R$ is a congruence relation on $G$.

    2. Quotient group. (4 points)

       The equivalence classes are: $[0]=\{0,3\}$, $[1]=\{1,4\}$, $[2]=\{2,5\}$. Thus $G/R=\{[0],[1],[2]\}$, with quotient addition $[x]+[y]=[x+_6 y]$:

       $$
       \begin{array}{c|ccc}
       + & [0] & [1] & [2] \\
       \hline
       [0] & [0] & [1] & [2] \\
       [1] & [1] & [2] & [0] \\
       [2] & [2] & [0] & [1]
       \end{array}
       $$

    3. All subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}_6$: $\{0\}$, $\{0,3\}$, $\{0,2,4\}$, $\mathbb{Z}_6$. (4 points)
</Solution>
