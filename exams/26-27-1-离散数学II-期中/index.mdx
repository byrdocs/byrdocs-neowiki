## 1.（8分）

Find $19^{42} \mod 41$.

## 2.（12分）

A box of candies, when grouped by 7, leaves 4 candies remaining. When grouped by 11, leaves 5 candies remaining. Find the smallest positive number of candies in this box.

## 3.（10分）

There are 7 students: 4 boys and 3 girls. Answer the following questions.

1. How many ways are there to select a committee of 4 students with at least one girl?
2. How many ways to arrange all 7 students in a row if the 3 girls must stand together as one block?

## 4.（6分）

We have unlimited red, yellow and blue balls. Answer the following questions.

1. How many ordered arrangements of 4 balls can we make?
2. How many unordered groups of 4 balls can we make?

## 5.（6分）

How many solutions does the equation $x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \le 10$ have, where $x_1 \ge 2$, $x_2$ and $x_3$ are nonnegative integers.

## 6.（8分）

Prove: If we pick 10 integers from the set $\{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 18\}$, then there must exist two selected integers such that one divides the other.

## 7.（10分）

Let $*$ be a binary operation on a set $A$. Suppose that for all $a, b, c \in A$, the following properties hold:

$$
\begin{aligned}
a * a &= a, \\
a * b &= b * a, \\
(a * b) * c &= a * (b * c).
\end{aligned}
$$

Define a relation $\le$ on $A$ by $a \le b \Leftrightarrow a * b = a$. Prove that the relation $\le$ has the following properties:

1. Prove that $\le$ is reflexive.
2. Prove that $\le$ is antisymmetric.
3. Prove that $\le$ is transitive.

## 8.（10分）

Let $A = \{0, 1\}$ and $A^{*}$ denote the set of all finite strings over $A$, including the empty string $\varepsilon$. The operation on $A^{*}$ is string concatenation, denoted by $\cdot$. Let $T = \{w \in A^{*} \mid \text{the number of 1s in } w \text{ is even}\}$.

1. Prove that $(A^{*}, \cdot)$ is a monoid, and identify its identity element.
2. Prove that $(T, \cdot)$ is a submonoid of $(A^{*}, \cdot)$.
3. Define the mapping $f \colon A^{*} \to \mathbb{Z}_{2}$, $f(w) = [\text{the number of 1s in } w]_{2}$. Prove that $f$ is a homomorphism from $A^{*}$ to $\mathbb{Z}_{2}$.

## 9.（10分）

Let $S = \{1, 2, 3, \ldots\}$ be the set of positive integers under the operation of ordinary addition. Define a relation $R$ on $S$ by $a \, R \, b$ if and only if $a \equiv b \pmod{3}$. Answer the following questions:

1. Prove that $R$ is a congruence relation on the semigroup $(S, +)$.
2. Define an operation $\oplus$ on $S/R$ by $[a] \oplus [b] = [a + b]$. Construct the operation table of the quotient semigroup $(S/R, \oplus)$.
3. Prove that $(S/R, \oplus)$ is a semigroup.

## 10.（10分）

Let $G = \mathbb{R}$ - $\{0\}$, and define a binary operation $*$ on $G$ by $a * b = ab/4$.

1. Prove that $(G, *)$ is an Abelian group.
2. Solve the equation $x * 4 = 10$ in $G$.

## 11.（10分）

Let $G = (\mathbb{Z}_{24}, +_{24})$ be the additive group of integers modulo 24, and let $H = \{[0], [8], [16]\}$.

1. Prove that $H$ is a subgroup of $G$.
2. Find the orders of $G$ and $H$, that is, determine $|G|$ and $|H|$.
